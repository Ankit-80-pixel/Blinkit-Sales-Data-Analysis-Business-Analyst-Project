# 🧾 Blinkit Sales Data Analysis – Business Analytics Project

## 📊 Project Overview
Comprehensive analysis of Blinkit sales data to identify key KPIs — **Total Sales, Average Sales, Items Sold, and Average Rating**.  
Performed detailed **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** and built **data visualizations** to uncover sales trends, outlet performance, and actionable business insights using Python.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives
- Understand sales performance across product categories and outlets.  
- Measure how **Fat Content, Item Type, Outlet Size, and Location** influence total sales.  
- Define and analyze **key performance indicators (KPIs)** to support strategic decision-making.

---

## 📈 Key KPIs
- **Total Sales:** Overall revenue generated.  
- **Average Sales:** Mean revenue per sale.  
- **Number of Items Sold:** Total count of distinct items sold.  
- **Average Rating:** Mean customer satisfaction score.

---

## 📉 Data Visualization
Visualizations created using **Matplotlib** and **Seaborn** to explore relationships between KPIs and business attributes:
- 🥧 **Donut Chart:** Total Sales by Fat Content  
- 📊 **Bar Chart:** Total Sales by Item Type  
- 🧱 **Stacked Column Chart:** Fat Content by Outlet for Total Sales  
- 📈 **Line Chart:** Total Sales by Outlet Establishment Year  
- 🍩 **Pie Chart:** Sales by Outlet Size  
- 🗺️ **Funnel Map:** Sales by Outlet Location  

---

## 💡 Insights
- Identified top-performing **item types and outlet categories** contributing most to revenue.  
- Found strong correlation between **outlet size and sales volume.**  
- Revealed customer preference trends through **average rating and product attributes.**  
- Delivered **data-backed recommendations** for improving retail performance.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
**Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Jupyter Notebook, CSV Data Processing**
